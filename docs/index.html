League keeper rules
  • A keeper costs one round higher than where he was drafted last season — a Rd 9 pick costs your Rd 8 pick.
  • A player who wasn't drafted last season costs a 16th-round pick.
  • A player can be kept for two seasons. The year you originally drafted him doesn't count.
  • If a keeper's cost would climb past Rd 1, he goes back into the draft pool.
  • Costs and eligibility follow the player, so a trade doesn't reset anything.
